Help & support

Good to know

What does a match score mean?

Dataset match scores are relative percentiles in the investor pool. They are not a promise or probability of investment.

Why can’t I send a request to every dataset investor?

A request needs a SponsorMatch account to reach. You can still prepare and copy a pitch for an investor who has not joined.

Can I edit the pitch?

Yes. The generated message is a starting point. Review and edit it before sending.

Where do I see replies?

Sign in and choose My requests. Investor accounts can accept or decline requests there.

Need help?
